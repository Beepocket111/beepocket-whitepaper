02INDUSTRY BACKGROUND & MARKET OPPORTUNITY

Market differences create
research space.

Digital Asset markets are distributed across multiple trading platforms, asset classes, and liquidity pools. Differences in prices, order-book depth, volume, fees, and execution mechanisms create scope for quantitative trading research and cross-market arbitrage.

Beepocket focuses on executable net spreads after trading fees, Slippage, fund-transfer costs, execution delays, platform restrictions, and market volatility—not on displayed price differences alone.

01

Data density

More market data creates more room for systematic analysis.

02

Execution speed

Automated systems can evaluate multiple markets continuously.

03

Risk discipline

Fees, liquidity, and volatility remain part of every decision.

05MULTI-MARKET ARBITRAGE STRATEGIES

Net spread,
not screen spread.

Cross-Exchange Arbitrage is the platform’s core business direction. Statistical arbitrage, liquidity analysis, and trading-cost management extend the strategy framework.
